02 · The verdict

The app is already good — the look is genuinely ownable and nothing else in the daily-game space resembles it. What it's missing is the growth loop. Wordle spread because the result you pasted into a chat was spoiler-free, recognisable, and a little smug. Dead Famous today shares plain numbers, only after all four games, with no link — and a pasted link unfurls as nothing (zero social meta tags). Research across twelve comparable games says the same thing: the share unit is the product's marketing department. Fix that loop, surface the streak, set the two one-way doors correctly (domain, epoch), and this has a real shot.

Installed iPhone PWAs can never migrate domains (no iOS mechanism; GitHub force-redirects once set). Whatever we launch under is forever. Decide now, wire it this week, launch under it. ~$15/yr.

Mondays have the easiest recipe — a winnable first puzzle for every stranger. EPOCH resets to this date. ~3 weeks out fits the plan.

Candlelit theme

Fully built in CSS, never turned on. Cheapest charm: auto-dim after local sunset ("the museum dims after dark") + a manual toggle. Or cut it for launch.

The stray files

~15 design-exploration files (this board included) sit untracked. Your standing "push = commit everything" rule would make them public on the live repo.

Streak repair

Today: miss a day, streak dies (1-day grace exists). Option: heal it by completing yesterday's issue from the archive within 48h — Duolingo-style forgiveness, big churn saver, slightly dilutes purity.

Face Value / Relic length

10 rounds ≈ 4–6 min each. Research favours 1–3 min dailies. Halving to 5 rounds doubles content runway but touches engine math. My honest rec: keep 10, let launch analytics decide.
