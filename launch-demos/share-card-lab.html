Share Card Lab

Proposal demo · how sharing could work · nothing here touches the app
Wordle grew on one trick: the result you paste into a chat is spoiler-free, instantly recognisable, and a little smug. Right now Dead Famous shares plain numbers, only after all four games, with no link. This lab shows the proposed fix: an emoji grammar per game (left — as your friends would see it in iMessage) plus a generated image receipt (right — for stories/feeds, made from the real brand kit).
