# Known-face audit of the live Face Value pool — 27 Jul 2026

Daniel's rule (27 Jul): Face Value requires a likeness the history-curious have
actually seen — photograph, famous painting, famous bust — or canonical
iconography for saints/religious figures. Test = image familiarity, not strict
authenticity. This audit reviewed all 80 pre-1500 portraits among the 426
Face Value items. Nothing moves without Daniel's sign-off.

## KEEP — 40 items, authentic or canonical (examples, not exhaustive)
Egyptian royals (Tut mask, Akhenaten, Ramesses II colossus, Hatshepsut statue);
Socrates (the snub-nosed bust IS philosophy's face), Alexander, Caesar,
Augustus (Prima Porta), Marcus Aurelius, Caracalla-class famous busts;
Justinian + Theodora (San Vitale mosaics, contemporary AND iconic);
Genghis Khan + Kublai Khan (Yuan court album = THE textbook images);
Dante (Botticelli profile), Henry V, Vlad (Ambras portrait), Joan of Arc
(armour/banner iconography), van Eyck (self-portrait), Vasco da Gama
(near-contemporary), Luther (Cranach from life), all self-portrait artists;
biblical/religious block via iconography (Jesus Pantocrator, Buddha, Moses'
tablets, Peter's keys, Paul's sword, Magdalene's skull, Madonna, Baptist).

## DISCUSS — Daniel's call, my lean noted
- confucius — Ma Yuan portrait is invented BUT arguably canonical like an
  icon. Lean: KEEP via iconography.
- plato (ed 48), aristotle (ed 46) — posthumous idealised busts, moderately
  reproduced, nothing like Socrates-famous. Lean: MOVE (plato has no map
  entry yet -> drop until built).
- vercingetorix — Royer painting, romantic invention. Lean: MOVE.
- cleopatra — bust is literally labelled "called Cleopatra"; real likeness =
  unflattering coins. BUT born AND died Alexandria -> zero-km, no Lifeline
  fallback. Lean: KEEP via death-scene iconography (asp paintings are
  canonical) and SWAP the image in Session B.
- boudica (ed 47) — Victorian statue invention. Lean: MOVE.
- timur — posthumous miniature, not widely familiar. Lean: MOVE (no map
  entry yet).
- montezuma-ii — European invention. Lean: MOVE (map exists).
- columbus, magellan, hernan-cortes — invented BUT the standard textbook
  faces everyone has seen. Lean: KEEP (familiarity test passes even though
  authenticity fails).
- david (ed 38), solomon (ed 44), abraham (ed 33) — iconography weaker than
  the rest of the biblical block (generic bearded king/patriarch). Lean:
  KEEP to preserve the block, but flagging honestly.
