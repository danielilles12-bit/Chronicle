Four small history games, every day. Free. No sign-up. No app to download — it just runs in the browser. A new set drops at midnight, the same four puzzles for everyone, whether you show up first or last. Built for people who already have a history podcast queue three deep: quick to start, harder to put down, allergic to padding.

The four games

  • Face Value

    A famous face, one scrap at a time.

  • Lifeline

    Born here, died there. Name the figure.

  • Relic

    A famous artefact, one scrap at a time.

  • Thread

    Group 16 clues into four hidden categories.
